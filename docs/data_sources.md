All CCMI-2022 refD1 records are published under the [Open Government Licence v3](http://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/); cite the dataset record listed in each `config/models/<model>.yaml`.

ACCESS-CM2-Chem refD1 (CEDA UUID 031313dc6bfc4a0baabd58a51629bc21) passed identifier verification and its raw files were archived, but it is excluded from the active dataset. Its archived halocarbon fields are inconsistent with the other sources: CFC-11 and CFC-12 mixing ratios sit at constant factors of about 2.5 and 1.4 above the other CCMI models at every level and season, which indicates a source-setup issue that post-processing cannot correct, and its terrain-following residual levels below roughly 560 hPa carry unresolved orographic deficits. The raw files are retained untracked under `data/raw/ACCESS-CM2-Chem/` for traceability; inventories, provenance, derived products and the site are built without this model.

NIWA-UKCA2 refD1 (CEDA UUID 9d93bed3b24648fcade5e427903c7da7) and UKESM1-StratTrop refD1 (CEDA UUID 2b93a4655e2645a898466c4ea9257c0e) passed identifier verification and their raw files were archived, but they are excluded from the active dataset. Their archived halocarbon fields are inconsistent with the other sources: NIWA-UKCA2 CFC-11 sits at a constant factor of about 2.5 above the other CCMI models at every level and season (CFC-12 about 1.4, methyl bromide about 1.9), and UKESM1-StratTrop CFC-11 about 2.6 (methyl bromide about 2.3), which indicates source-setup issues that post-processing cannot correct; UKESM1-StratTrop additionally publishes total-ozone column values near 2.8e7 m, three orders of magnitude above physical values. The raw files are retained untracked under `data/raw/NIWA-UKCA2/` and `data/raw/UKESM1-StratTrop/` for traceability; inventories, provenance, derived products and the site are built without these models.

## Source coordinate conventions

The WACCM-X archive publishes ready-made monthly zonal `_zm.nc` files; JuMACS uses these directly. Its file `time` coordinate is the **next month's first day**, while `time_bnds` and the filename identify the represented month. The reader labels samples with the interval midpoint, so `1985-01_zm.nc` contributes to January 1985. Its 126 hybrid midpoint levels use `p=hyam*P0+hybm*PS`. GEOSCCM full 3-D fields use `p=a*p0+b*ps` (72 levels); EMAC uses `p=ap+b*ps` (90 levels). CCMI `AmonZ` chemistry uses native pressure coordinates in Pa. Actual valid coverage is calculated from finite species values and pressure, not inferred from model top. Altitude comes from native geopotential height where coordinates align, particularly for WACCM-X. Other levels receive a labelled `7 ln(101325/p)` km scale-height estimate, which is unreliable above roughly 80 km.

## Registry

`config/models/` is the source registry: one YAML file per CCMI-2022 refD1 model plus `config/waccmx.yaml` for the separate whole-atmosphere source. GEOSCCM, EMAC and the six other CCMI refD1 models whose CEDA identifiers were verified live against the archive and catalogue (CCSR-NIES-MIROC32, CESM2-WACCM, CMAM, CNRM-MOCAGE, IPSL-CM6A-ATM-LR-REPROBUS, SOCOL) are `status: ready` with `archive_inventory` and `download` enabled. MIROC-ES2H stays `status: unresolved`: it has no refD1 contribution in the archive or catalogue (its collection publishes refD2 and sensitivity experiments only), so no identifier was guessed. Identifiers are never guessed; each ready entry carries a comment recording the dataset record contact, publication year and verification date. SOCOL publishes a non-standard layout (member directories `gn`/`gnz`, grid directory `r1i1p1f1`); the inventory walker accepts both `YYYYMM` and `YYYYMMDD` monthly timestamp forms. Each source also declares explicit `capabilities` (`archive_inventory`, `download`, `zonal_processing`, `climatology`, `compact_waccmx`); commands such as `compact` accept only models whose capability is enabled, and unregistered capability defaults to false. `config/species.yaml` defines the canonical species list and groups used by the inventory and coverage matrices. `inspect --model all` writes model-specific archive inventories plus `products/comparison/variable_matrix.csv` (per-model `<model>_status` of `ok`/`absent`/`no_mapping`/`unresolved`/`not_inspected` and `<model>_available`) and `products/comparison/native_variables.csv` across the full registry. `jumacs summary` then works fully offline on the local inventories and regenerates `products/comparison/model_summary.csv` (per-model file counts, time range, Amon/AmonZ variable counts, mapped-species counts), `products/comparison/species_summary.csv` (per-species model coverage, AmonZ-only counts, WACCM-X availability) and `products/comparison/summary.md` (overview, coverage, per-group chemistry richness, Amon/AmonZ split, gaps, and a separate WACCM-X section). The eight newly verified CCMI models publish the standard CCMI native variable names and their `variables:` mappings follow the already-mapped GEOSCCM/EMAC names. The native-variable report keeps source fields that exist only in a monthly multi-variable file, such as WACCM-X native species, separate from the canonical species list until a mapping is deliberately configured. Missing species remain missing. No family sum replaces an individual gas. Neither CCMI refD1 source has a monthly SF6 directory, and the inspected WACCM-X zonal file has no three-dimensional SF6 field. WACCM-X zonal files contain many variables together, so selecting one variable for download still selects the monthly multi-variable files.

When using the data, cite the CEDA dataset records and follow their licences; the MIT license covers the project code.
